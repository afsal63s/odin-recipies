Lasagna

Description

        Lasagna is a classic Italian-American baked pasta dish made with layers of wide,
        flat pasta sheets, a rich and savory meat sauce (often with tomatoes), creamy béchamel sauce,
        and generous amounts of cheese like ricotta and mozzarella, all baked together until bubbly and golden brown.

        Beyond the fundamental layers, lasagna offers a comforting and hearty meal with a delightful combination of textures and flavors.
        The tender pasta softens in the sauce, while the meat sauce provides a deep, savory richness complemented by the smooth and creamy béchamel.
        Finally, the melted and often slightly browned cheese on top adds a satisfyingly gooey and flavorful finish, making each bite a truly satisfying experience.
